Blinded License

The Blinded license allows you to redact documents that are more than 20 pages.

· I lost my license

Your license is kept in this browser. Take a copy of it to use Blinded on another machine.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to cancel. A license is a string. When it runs out it stops working, and that is the end of it.

Works offline

Verified on your own machine against a key that can check a license and cannot make one. Using it tells us nothing — not that you used it, not when, not what you were redacting.

Your file is not involved

Buying happens on a separate page, in a separate window. The page holding your document still cannot reach a payment processor, or anything else.
