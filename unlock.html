A pass for Blinded

Everything in Blinded is free. A document longer than twenty pages needs a pass to write the finished file.

    What you are buying

    • A pass, not an account. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to cancel. When it runs out it stops working, and that is the whole of it.
    • It is a string. You get it here and by email. Paste it into Blinded on any machine, as many times as you like, until it expires.
    • It is checked on your machine. Blinded holds a key that can verify a pass but cannot make one. Nothing is sent anywhere to check it, so it works offline, and using it tells us nothing — not that you used it, not when, and not what you were redacting.
    • Your document is not here. This page is not the tool. It has never seen your file and cannot: the file is in the other tab and never leaves it.

    Here it is

    Copied into Blinded automatically. This is also in your email — keep it somewhere, and it will work on any machine until it expires.

    Anything goes wrong

    Write to support@blinded.dev. If a payment went through and you have no pass, say so and include the email address you paid with – that is ours to fix, not yours.

    Press I already have a pass in Blinded and paste it in. You do not need this page.
