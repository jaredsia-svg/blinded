Premium

Almost all of Blinded is free, and the part that is free is the part that matters. What is paid for is length.

    Get a pass

    What is free, at any length, forever

    • The redaction itself. Every word, every logo, every detector, the reading of scanned pages, the second check that looks for words which exist only as pictures — all of it, on a document of any size.
    • Opening, searching, marking and reviewing. You can do the entire job on a five-hundred-page file and see everything it found before any question of money comes up.
    • Saving a draft. Your marks and the words you typed, as a file on your own machine, to reopen and carry on later.
    • Exporting anything up to twenty pages. A contract, a letter, a scan of something that came in the post — the jobs somebody does once and would not pay for.

    What a pass is for

    One thing: writing the finished file for a document longer than twenty pages. That is the whole of it. There is no feature behind it, no better matcher, no faster search.

    It is deliberate that the line is drawn at length rather than at quality. A redaction tool that redacts better if you pay is selling safety, and safety is the one thing that must not be metered. The free version and the paid version remove exactly the same things, exactly as thoroughly.

    How a pass works

    • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, nothing to cancel. A pass is a string. When it runs out it stops working, and that is the end of it.
    • No subscription. Neither pass renews. You buy five days or a month, and then you have bought nothing else.
    • It works offline. Blinded holds a key that can check a pass and cannot make one, so it is verified on your own machine. Nothing is sent anywhere to confirm it — which means using it tells us nothing: not that you used it, not when, and not what you were redacting.
    • It travels. The same string works on any machine you paste it into, as many times as you like, until it expires.
    • Your document is never involved. Buying happens on a separate page, in a separate window. The page holding your file is forbidden by the browser from talking to a payment processor, and that has not changed to make room for this.

    Questions

    What happens when my pass runs out?

    Long documents stop exporting. Everything else keeps working, and every file you already exported is yours. Nothing is taken away and nothing is held hostage — your draft, your marks and your documents are on your machine, not ours.

    Can I try it first?

    You already can, on the document you actually care about. Open a five-hundred-page file, redact all of it, and look at every mark. You are only asked for money at the last step, and only if the file is long.

    I bought a pass and lost it.

    It is in the email from the purchase. If you cannot find it, write to support@blinded.dev with the email address you paid with.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within fourteen days, for any reason. Paddle is the merchant of record and handles it — or write to support@blinded.dev and we will.

    Do you need a licence for a team or a firm?

    Write to support@blinded.dev. Blinded is source-available and runs on your own machines, so a firm that wants to host it internally is a conversation rather than a product page.
