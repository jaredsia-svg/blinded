Premium

Almost all of Blinded is free. What is paid for is length.

Get a pass

No account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to cancel. A pass is a string. When it runs out it stops working, and that is the end of it.

Works offline

Verified on your own machine against a key that can check a pass and cannot make one. Using it tells us nothing — not that you used it, not when, not what you were redacting.

Your file is not involved

Buying happens on a separate page, in a separate window. The page holding your document still cannot reach a payment processor, or anything else.

The line is length, not quality

A pass buys one thing: writing the finished file for a document longer than twenty pages. There is no feature behind it — no better matcher, no faster search. The free version and the paid version remove exactly the same things, exactly as thoroughly, the second check included.

That is deliberate. A redaction tool that redacts better if you pay is selling safety, and safety is the one thing that must not be metered.
